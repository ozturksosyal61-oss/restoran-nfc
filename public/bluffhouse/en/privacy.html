Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the “Bluff House” mobile game (the “App”). The App is intended for users aged 18 and over.

1. Data controller

OZT Digital (developer: Turan Öztürk), Üsküdar, Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: oztdigitaldestek@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Account data: the username and password you choose. Your password is held by the Unity Authentication service and is not visible to us. A Player ID is assigned to your account. If you play as a guest, no account is created.
  • Gameplay data: your chosen character, the room and room code you join, and moves during a game (only for the duration of the game).
  • Safety data: your list of blocked players and the reports you submit (reported player's username and ID, reason, optional note, time, room code).
  • Voice chat: if you grant microphone permission, your voice is transmitted in real time to other players in the room. We do not record or store your voice.
  • Friends feature (signed-in users only): your Player ID and username, friend, request and block relationships, your online/offline status (presence, last seen) and the room invitations (room code) you send to friends are processed by the Unity Gaming Services Friends service.
  • Purchase records: a record of the items you buy (for example a character or accessory pack) is kept with your account in Cloud Save and on your device. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play; we do not see or store your card or payment details. The accessories you equip are visible to other players during a game.
  • Technical data: IP address (and the approximate location, such as country or region, that can be derived from it), device and operating system information needed to connect to servers; processed by Unity, Photon and the relevant store services to operate the service. We do not collect your precise (GPS) location.

3. Data we do not collect

Email address, phone number, real name, precise (GPS) location, contacts, camera or photos, advertising ID. The App has no ads, uses no ad networks or marketing behavioural analytics, and we do not sell your data.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating accounts, signing you in and providing multiplayer games (performance of a contract).
  • Keeping usernames unique, preventing harassment and cheating, reviewing reports and keeping the community safe (legitimate interests).
  • Voice chat: the microphone permission you grant on your device (consent), which you can withdraw in your device settings at any time.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

5. Sharing and international transfers

  • Other players: your username and character are visible in the room, and your voice is audible if your microphone is on.
  • Unity Technologies (Authentication and Cloud Save): account and saved data.
  • Exit Games (Photon Realtime and Photon Voice): room, game message and voice chat traffic.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): processing and restoring purchases.

These providers' servers may be located outside Türkiye (for example in Europe and the United States). Data is transferred only as needed to provide the service and in line with applicable law. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

6. Microphone and voice chat

In the App you can mute other players, block a player or report them. The room owner can remove a player from the room.

7. Retention

Account data, friend records and your purchase record are kept until you delete your account (your purchase history in your store account remains with the relevant store). You can delete it inside the App (see Delete Account). Report records are kept for as long as needed for safety review. In-room game data ceases to exist when the game ends.

8. Security

Your data is protected by our service providers' security measures. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure; do not share your password.

9. Your rights

Under Turkish law (KVKK Art. 11) and, if you are in the EU/EEA, the GDPR, you may ask whether your data is processed, request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, and complain to the competent data protection authority. Email oztdigitaldestek@gmail.com with your username; we reply within 30 days.

10. Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18 and will delete any such account we become aware of.

11. Changes

If we add new features, this policy will be updated. The current version is published on this page and the date above changes.
