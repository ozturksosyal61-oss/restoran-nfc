FAQ

I forgot my password

We do not collect email, so password reset is not available at the moment. If you cannot access your account you can create a new one, and follow the steps on the Delete Account page to remove the old one.

How do I report or block a player?

In a room, tap the player's seat or their name in the “Players” panel; you will see Block, Report and (if you are the room owner) Remove from room. Manage blocked players in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete Account.

How do I add a friend?

On the Friends screen, enter your friend's username together with their code (for example ahmet#1234). Your own code is shown at the top of the screen and can be copied. The friends feature is for signed-in users.

My purchase is not showing

Tap “Restore purchases” on the store screen and make sure you are signed in with the App Store / Google account you used to buy. If it persists, email support.

Voice chat does not work

Enable the microphone permission for Bluff House in your device settings and tap “Unmute” in the App.
