Account and Data Deletion

Bluff House · Last updated: November 5, 2026

You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: Inside the app (recommended, immediate)

  1. Open Bluff House and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings and go to the Account section.
  3. Tap “Delete account” and confirm.

Option 2: If you cannot sign in

Email oztdigitaldestek@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include your username and any details that help show the account is yours (for example sign-up date, the device you used). Because we do not collect email addresses, we may be unable to delete an account when we cannot verify ownership, in order to protect other people's accounts. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted?

  • Your account, username and Player ID.
  • Saved data linked to your account: your block list and the reports you submitted.
  • Local session data on your device.

Deletion cannot be undone. Copies in technical backups are removed within a reasonable period. We do not record voice chat, so there are no voice recordings to delete.

For privacy questions see the Privacy Policy.
